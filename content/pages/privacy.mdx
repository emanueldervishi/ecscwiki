---
title: Privacy policy
description: How the ECSC Albania wiki handles visitor information.
---

The wiki is a static documentation site. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

## What is collected

The wiki does not ask for or store personal information. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, request time, and requested page, to keep the site secure and running.

## Cookies and local storage

The site stores your theme preference (light or dark) in your own browser. Nothing is sent back to the team.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised with a team administrator.
