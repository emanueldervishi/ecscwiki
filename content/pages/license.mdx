---
title: Content and usage policy
description: Rules for using and contributing to the ECSC Albania wiki.
---

The ECSC Albania wiki contains public guidance and team-restricted material. Access does not grant permission to republish private, embargoed, or third-party content.

## Contributions

Contributors confirm that they have the right to share submitted material and that it does not contain live flags, credentials, personal data, private keys, or event content that remains under embargo.

## Authorized use

Technical material is provided for education, approved competitions, and systems where the user has explicit authorization. It must not be used to access or disrupt systems without permission.

## Third-party material

Challenge statements, binaries, trademarks, and linked references remain the property of their respective owners and are subject to their original terms.

## Corrections

Contact the team maintainers to report content that is inaccurate, unsafe, improperly attributed, or published outside its permitted scope.
