The archive organizes solved challenges by event, year, category, and reusable technique. It should help a teammate answer: “Have we seen this pattern before?”

## Index fields

Every archived entry should include:

- Event and year.
- Challenge name and category.
- Difficulty or point value when meaningful.
- Primary and supporting techniques.
- Contributors.
- Artifact availability and publication status.
- Link to the reproducible solver and write-up.

## Technique tags

Use specific tags such as `ret2libc`, `tcache-poisoning`, `idor`, `ssti`, `lattice`, `nonce-reuse`, `volatility`, or `pcap`. Avoid vague tags such as `hard`, `interesting`, or `misc` when a technical description is possible.

## Publication status

Mark every entry as **internal**, **embargoed**, or **public**. Event rules and author permission determine whether artifacts and solutions may leave the team workspace.

## Quality bar

Before indexing a write-up, verify it follows the [write-up standard](/docs/operations/writeup-standard), runs from a clean environment, and does not contain secrets or live flags.
