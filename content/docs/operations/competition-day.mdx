title: Competition-day playbook
description: The team checklist for preparation, challenge ownership, escalation, submissions, and post-event capture.

---

Use this playbook for jeopardy-style events unless the competition lead publishes an event-specific override.

## Before the start

- Verify accounts, multi-factor authentication, VPN access, and time synchronization.
- Confirm primary and backup communication channels.
- Start from clean, updated, isolated workspaces.
- Assign category leads, an operations lead, and a submission owner.
- Review event rules, flag format, scope, scoring, and prohibited tools.
- Prepare the shared challenge board and artifact structure.

## Opening scan

Spend the first minutes reading broadly. Category leads should tag obvious warmups, dependency-heavy tasks, and challenges that fit known strengths. Avoid having the whole team pile onto the first visible task.

## During the event

1. Claim work before deep investigation.
2. Save original artifacts and record their hashes.
3. Post material progress at agreed intervals.
4. Escalate blockers with a reproducible state.
5. Verify flags carefully before submission.
6. Preserve the final solver and exact environment.

## Submission safety

The submission owner checks the challenge name, flag format, and source before submitting. Never paste a flag into general chat or a permanent document.

## Final hour

Review every active challenge. Consolidate promising partial work, reassign specialists, and stop low-value branches. Make sure solved challenges have saved artifacts even if the full write-up comes later.

## After the event

Within 24 hours, archive permitted artifacts, assign write-ups, record operational lessons, rotate event-specific credentials, and schedule a blameless review.
