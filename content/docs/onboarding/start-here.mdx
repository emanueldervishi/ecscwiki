This checklist gets a new member from invitation to a safe, repeatable training setup.

## First day checklist

- Read the [code of conduct](/docs/onboarding/code-of-conduct).
- Confirm your display name and contact details in the team dashboard.
- Join the approved communication channels with the same recognizable identity.
- Choose one primary category and one secondary category.
- Prepare a dedicated virtual machine through the [lab access guide](/docs/infrastructure/lab-access).
- Install the [core tooling](/docs/infrastructure/tooling) for your primary track.
- Complete one baseline challenge and publish a short write-up.

## Training rhythm

Use a simple weekly loop:

1. Pick one clearly scoped skill.
2. Study the underlying concept before searching for a payload.
3. Solve one or more challenges that exercise the skill.
4. Write down the commands, failed assumptions, and final reasoning.
5. Explain the solution to another teammate.

## Ask for help well

When blocked, share enough context for another member to reproduce your state:

```text title="Blocker update"
Challenge: heap-notes
Goal: control the forward pointer after free
Confirmed: glibc 2.35, PIE + NX, no UAF crash
Tried: tcache poisoning with safe-linking reversal
Need: second pair of eyes on the leak primitive
Artifacts: /team/challenges/heap-notes/member-name/
```

## Safety boundary

Use team methods only in authorized labs, competitions, and systems where you have explicit permission. If scope is unclear, stop and ask a coach.
