title: Code of conduct
description: The behavior, safety, and collaboration standard for the ECSC Albania team.

---

ECSC Albania is a competitive team and a learning community. Members are expected to combine technical ambition with respect, discipline, and care for one another.

## Team expectations

- Treat teammates, opponents, organizers, volunteers, and mentors with respect.
- Give credit for ideas, discoveries, tooling, and write-ups.
- Make room for questions and explain concepts without humiliation or gatekeeping.
- Challenge technical claims with evidence, not personalities.
- Respect competition rules, embargoes, licenses, and authorized scope.
- Protect team infrastructure, credentials, private discussions, and personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, deliberate sabotage, credential sharing, cheating, unauthorized access, and publication of protected material are incompatible with team membership.

## Reporting concerns

Raise a concern privately with a coach or designated team lead. Reports should be handled discreetly, documented proportionately, and escalated when safety or competition integrity requires it.

## Competition integrity

Use only resources allowed by the event rules. Do not exchange flags or solutions with other teams. Do not attack event infrastructure, scoring systems, or competitors unless a challenge explicitly authorizes that activity.
